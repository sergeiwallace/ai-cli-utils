- A launch's bounded waits now retry under one policy, the `[retry]` table of `config.toml`: a number of attempts, each with a timeout that grows by `growth` up to `max_timeout_seconds`, with a jittered wait between them. It is read on every call, so an edit applies to the next launch, and `[retry.<site>]` overrides it for one site. The wake-sidecar ensure is the first site: it used to give up after a fixed 3 s, which a loaded host overran, and now makes two attempts, of 4 and then 6 s within a 12 s total, before it reports the attempt count and the last cause and lets the launch continue.
