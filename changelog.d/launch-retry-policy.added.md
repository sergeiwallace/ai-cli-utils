- A launch's bounded waits now retry under one policy, the `[retry]` table of `config.toml`: how many attempts, a per-attempt timeout that grows by `growth` up to `max_timeout_seconds`, and a jittered delay between attempts. It is read on every call, so an edit applies to the next launch, and `[retry.<site>]` overrides it for one site. The wake-sidecar ensure is the first site: it used to give up after a fixed 3 s, which a loaded host overran, and now retries with 5, 10 and 20 s before it reports the attempt count and the last cause and lets the launch continue.
