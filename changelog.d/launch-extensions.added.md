- `ai c` runs launch extensions: commands listed under `[extensions.run.<name>]` in config.toml run once before the session starts, in a directory that may be absolute or relative to `[project] projects_dir`. `on_error` (a default in `[extensions]`, overridable per extension) decides whether a failure warns and launches or refuses the launch; every attempt is recorded in the launch log. Nothing configured changes nothing.
