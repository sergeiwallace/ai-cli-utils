- The optional Dolt supervisor's ensure before a launch now has a timeout, `[retry.dolt_server]` (120 s, asked once). A hung ensure used to hold the launch with no bound; now the launch says it did not finish and continues, while a server it asked the unit manager to start keeps starting. A wake-sidecar ensure or show lookup also costs a launch at most `[retry.wake_sidecar] max_total_seconds` (10 s), however many attempts it is allowed.
