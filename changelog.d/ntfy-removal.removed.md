- The ntfy notification channel is gone. `ai quota watch` and every other notification now go to the Discord (or Slack) webhook, with the OS desktop alert as the fallback. `NTFY_BASE_URL`, `NTFY_TOPIC` and `NTFY_TOKEN` are no longer read, `ai notifications list` no longer shows an ntfy row, and a leftover `[notifications.ntfy]` section in `config.toml` is ignored without a warning.
