- A launch or restart no longer prints "background resolver exited ...; resuming by title instead" when `[relaunch] background_session_resolver` reports that nothing is pending. Exit status 3 is now that report: it is recorded in the launch log with the resolver's last stderr line and stays off the terminal. Any other non-zero exit, 1 included, is still a warning, now quoting the resolver's last stderr line, and every such warning also reaches the launch log.
