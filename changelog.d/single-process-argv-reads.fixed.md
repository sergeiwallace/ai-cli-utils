- On macOS, a `SystemError` from psutil reading one specific process's arguments or environment (`sysctl`) no longer crashes `ai -X` session restart, `ai sessions enrol`, `ai tunnel start`/`stop` or session exit. Each check that decides whether to signal, restart or record a process now treats an unreadable process as unproven and refuses, with its usual message; the restart lookup for a pane's `claude attach` client skips that process and reads the others.
